network-asset

Spec 110-network-assets: a device a router's controller manages, as an asset of the fleet: adopted through the integration (no SSH, no probe), recorded in Git, tagged and shown like any machine, and read (never changed) from the controller's observation.

  • The record (fleets/<fleet>/adopted/<name>/network.ts, networkAssetSource, loadNetworkAssets) is who it is: the connector it came through (via) and that connector's account, the device's id, MAC and serial (where the API gives one), its class and model, when it was adopted, auto: true where a fleet rule did it, and the tags a person gave it. loadAdopted (SSH machines) does not read it.
  • The rules (fleets/<fleet>/network.ts, defineNetwork): autoAdopt rules, typed by the ontology's device classes and validated at build (networkProblems); estate observe applies them (autoAdoptChoices), writing records that stolos apply commits.
  • The facts (firmware, address, state) are the connector's observation (observedDevices, observedClients), read at every use. reconcile says for each asset whether it is present, gone from the integration (a warning, never a deletion) or unread.

Source: src/archon/connect/network-asset.ts
