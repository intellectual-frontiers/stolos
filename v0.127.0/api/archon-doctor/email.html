email

archon doctor's email checks (spec 026, for specs 025 and 027), shared by servers and the boot machine: the sending domain's records in DNS, the relay's credential, a login to the relay that sends nothing, and on servers Postfix itself. The records are the ones Cloudflare's Email Sending onboarding creates (developers.cloudflare.com/email-service/configuration/domains/). With send (archon doctor --send, and by itself after mail-setup, an install, or a new token), it also mails what it found to the shared mailbox through each path: archon's own sender, and on servers Postfix.

Source: src/archon/doctor/email.ts

Which of Cloudflare Email Sending's records a domain lacks, from its TXT and MX answers.

domainRecordsfunction
function domainRecords(domain: string): Promise<string[]>

Looks up a sending domain's SPF, DKIM, DMARC, and bounce MX in DNS and returns the ones missing.

emailChecksfunction
function emailChecks(site: Site | undefined, phase: Phase, fixCredential: string, o?: { send?: string | undefined; }): Promise<void>

The Email phase of archon doctor: the sending domain's records, a login to the relay with its credential (fixCredential is the fix shown when it is missing or refused), and on servers Postfix. With send, also mails the findings. Reports into phase; does nothing when the site has no mail.
