schema

Spec 080: the tables the risk register and the vendor inventory add to the estate graph, declared once in spec 089's schema language. Every name is a cited standard's term: risk and poam_item are OSCAL's (a risk, and a plan of action item: an accepted risk is a deviation approved until a date); data_classification is OCSF's (what kind of data a vendor holds). Vendors themselves are the estate's own UDM parties: an organization with the party_role supplier, joined to what revealed it by an edge (supplies).

Source: src/archon/risk/schema.ts
