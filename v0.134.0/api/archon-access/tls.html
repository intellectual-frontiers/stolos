HTTPS on every server

tls

Spec 100 FR-002 and FR-003: the console's DNS records, its certificate, and the addresses it may listen on.

  • DNS (Cloudflare, the token of boot web-setup): an A record to this PC's LAN address (not proxied) for <pc>.stolos.<domain> and, on the primary, stolos.<domain>; and the heartbeat, a TXT record _stolos-console.stolos.<domain> = "<pc> <ISO time>", every 5 minutes.
  • The certificate (ACME, the DNS challenge): <pc>.stolos.<domain>, *.stolos.<domain>, and stolos.<domain> on the primary. It, its key and the ACME account are one secret in the vault (console/tls.json), renewed when 30 days or fewer remain.
  • Article 28: only a private (RFC 1918) or mesh (100.64.0.0/10) address is ever bound.

Source: src/archon/access/tls.ts
