archon/estate/schema.ts

Spec 090: the estate graph's schema, declared once (spec 089's schema language) and generated into the canonical SQL, the docs, and the views. Physical tables follow the Universal Data Model (parties, people, roles, contact mechanisms, fixed assets) and a property graph (tenant, node, label, edge); views carry OCSF's and D3FEND's terms. Every name is cited.

flowchart LR
  tenant --> node --> node_label
  node --- edge
  party --> person & organization
  person --- party_contact_mechanism --- contact_mechanism
  person --- party_fixed_asset_assignment --- fixed_asset
  node --> user_account & code_repository
  person --> user_inventory
  fixed_asset --> device
