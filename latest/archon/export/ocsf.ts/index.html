archon/export/ocsf.ts

Spec 092: the estate and the compliance view as OCSF 1.9.0 events, one JSON object per line (JSON Lines), for a SIEM or a data lake to read without a parser of its own. Every event is written by IFC TypeScript's kernel (ocsf), typed by its OCSF 1.9.0 types, so a missing required attribute does not compile; the mapping from the estate's rows is Stolos's.

flowchart LR
  estate[("estate graph")] --> people["5003 per person, then their accounts"]
  estate --> devices["5001 per fixed asset"]
  estate --> nodes["5023 per other node"]
  view["compliance view"] --> findings["2003 per evidence and SCF control"]
  people & devices & nodes & findings --> jsonl["ocsf.jsonl"]

Only what the estate already holds goes out: a person's name, work email, status, role, dates, and manager; an account's name, email, and flags; a machine's names, serial number, operating system, and owner's work email. An account is tied to its person by metadata.correlation_uid (the person's uid). Activity is 2 (Collect): Stolos collected it from the sources; severity is 1 (Informational) for inventory.
