archon/console/auth.ts

Spec 065: who is signed in to the console, and what they may do.

  • A session is a random id in an HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Strict cookie, held in memory (a restart signs everyone out), ended after 8 idle hours.
  • The accounts are the server's own (PAM): people with a home under the normal user range. root and ops (Archon's service account) never sign in here, as Cockpit refuses root.
  • Roles: an operator (in sudo or archon-ops) may run commands that change the server; everyone else may look. Destroying data also needs the password again and the command's typed confirmation.
  • Five failed sign-ins in a minute from one address pause that address.
