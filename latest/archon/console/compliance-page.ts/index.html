archon/console/compliance-page.ts

Spec 068: the Compliance tab, in both consoles: each SCF control with its evidence, and each framework's requirements (SOC 2, HIPAA, PCI DSS, CMMC, NIST SP 800-53 and 800-171, FedRAMP) with what their controls say, through the SCF Council's own mappings (spec 071). Without the catalog on this machine, the page says so and shows the controls alone. Spec 073: each control shows its evidence's age, and stale evidence carries a stale pill.
