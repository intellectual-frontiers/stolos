archon/console/pages.ts

Spec 065: the Archon Console's pages, as typed functions of their data: sign-in, Overview, Actions, Activity, and Guide. Written for developers who have not run servers before: each page says what it is for, and the diagrams (Mermaid, drawn from this server's real state) show how the pieces connect: the fleet repository, signing, the 15-minute convergence, and what happens when you press Run.
