archon/capabilities/smb.ts

037-smb: file shares over SMB (Samba) on every server, off until an operator turns them on with archon fs smb on; then on, across reboots and reinstalls, until archon fs smb off. The switch is the host's own recorded state (the host's own archon.sql (opt-in smb)), which every apply honors.

Overrides of Ubuntu defaults: smbd masked until switched on (Ubuntu starts it on install); nmbd always masked (NetBIOS off).
