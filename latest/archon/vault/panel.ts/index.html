archon/vault/panel.ts

The backup panel (spec 029, Article 19 and 22): one framed panel, amber, red when anything has been unbacked for more than 7 days or host identity keys were never backed up. Shown at the end of any command that changed a secret, and at the start of boot serve and boot status, until the secrets are backed up. Names only.
