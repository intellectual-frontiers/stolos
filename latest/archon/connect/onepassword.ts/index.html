archon/connect/onepassword.ts

Spec 087: 1Password (Business or Enterprise Password Manager), read-only, for one account: its people (active, suspended, or not yet joined), read with the 1Password Users API and an OAuth application that holds only the users.view scope. The estate graph joins them to the roster: every active person has a 1Password account (coverage), and nobody who left still has an active one. Evidence for account management (SCF IAC-08).

1Password's limits: the Users API shows neither who is an owner or administrator, nor two-factor status, nor vaults and who can open them (1Password keeps those in its admin console, its command line tool, and its SDKs, which act with a person's or a service account's own access, never read-only). It is in public preview, works with Business and Enterprise accounts only, and not alongside automated provisioning (SCIM).
