archon/connect/roster.ts

Spec 091: what every HR connector declared with the SDK shares. An HR system gives the roster only (the source of truth for who should have access): work email, name, status, start and end dates, manager, and role type. Never compensation, government ids, addresses, birth dates, or other personal fields: each connector reduces the vendor's record to a {@link RosterRow} inside its list, so nothing else leaves it.

Evidence for account management (SCF IAC-08) and provisioning (IAC-04): the estate graph checks every account against the roster.
