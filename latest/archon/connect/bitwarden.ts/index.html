archon/connect/bitwarden.ts

Spec 087: Bitwarden (Password Manager, Teams or Enterprise), read-only, for one organization: its members (confirmed, invited, revoked; owner or admin; whether two-step login is on), whether the organization requires two-step login, and its collections (the shared vaults) with who can open each, directly or through a group. Evidence for MFA (SCF IAC-37), least privilege (IAC-30), and account management (IAC-08); the estate graph checks that every active person has an account (coverage).

Bitwarden's organization API key is not read-only (Bitwarden documents it as full access to the organization's management API): Stolos sends it only to the token endpoint and makes GET calls alone. Item names and contents are end-to-end encrypted and never readable through this API.
