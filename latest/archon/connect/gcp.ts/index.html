archon/connect/gcp.ts

Spec 088: Google Cloud, read-only, for one project: its resources (Cloud Asset Inventory, assets.list), who holds which role on the project (Cloud Asset Inventory's searchAllIamPolicies, a GET), its service accounts and their user-managed keys (IAM), and where its logs are routed (Cloud Logging's sinks). Evidence for least privilege (SCF IAC-30), public access (DCH-02), long-lived keys (IAC-10), and audit logging (MON-03).

Credential: a service account's JSON key, with the read-only roles Cloud Asset Viewer (roles/cloudasset.viewer), Security Reviewer (roles/iam.securityReviewer), and Logs Viewer (roles/logging.viewer) on the project; it signs in with the scope cloud-platform.read-only (the JWT is signed here, RS256, as for Google Workspace). Docs: https://cloud.google.com/asset-inventory/docs/reference/rest, https://cloud.google.com/iam/docs/reference/rest, and https://cloud.google.com/logging/docs/reference/v2/rest.
