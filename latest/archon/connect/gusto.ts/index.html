archon/connect/gusto.ts

Spec 091: Gusto (payroll and HR), read-only, the roster only: each employee's work email, name, whether they have left (and when), hire date, manager, and employment status. Never compensation, SSNs, addresses, birth dates, or bank details: Gusto's employee record has them, and they are dropped as it is read. Evidence for account management (SCF IAC-08) and provisioning (IAC-04), through the estate graph.

Gusto's API is OAuth 2.0 for registered apps (dev.gusto.com): a company's access token, scope employees:read, lasts two hours, and its refresh token is single-use. There is no self-serve, long-lived read-only token.
