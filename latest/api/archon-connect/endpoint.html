endpoint

Spec 087 (part A): what endpoint management (MDM) and endpoint detection and response (EDR) connectors share. Each reads its devices as hardware_device records (serial number, hostname, operating system, owner, disk encryption, last check-in, and an EDR's sensor health); the estate graph merges them with Stolos's own probe by serial number, then hostname, so each device is counted once. This module turns those records into posture facts and the policies every such connector checks:

  • every device encrypts its disk (SCF CRY-07);
  • every device checked in within CHECK_IN_DAYS days (AST-04, MON-02);
  • every EDR sensor is present and healthy (END-04, MON-02);
  • every device meets its MDM's compliance policy (CFG-04), where the MDM reports it.

Source: src/archon/connect/endpoint.ts

CHECK_IN_DAYSconst
const CHECK_IN_DAYS: 14

A device that has not checked in for this many days is stale.

devicePosturefunction
function devicePosture(records: ObservedRecord[], now: Date): Record<string, number>

The posture facts of a connector's devices: how many, and how many are unencrypted, stale, have an unhealthy sensor, or are not compliant. A fact is left out when no device reports it (the policy is then unknown, not passing).
