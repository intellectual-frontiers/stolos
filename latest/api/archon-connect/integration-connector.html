integration-connector

Spec 109-integrations-api FR-003, FR-006: the integration layer as a family of connectors (spec 084). integrationConnector makes a Connector of integrations, so a router, a DNS provider or an email provider is connected, observed, checked and shown like any account: a read-only credential in the vault, stolos connect <id>, an observation in fleets/<fleet>/estate/<id>.ts, posture policies with SCF controls, gaps reported rather than failing. withIntegrations gives a connector that already exists (Cloudflare) the kinds it can also be, without changing a line of it.

What an integration reads becomes the one estate graph (spec 085, 090): records with their labels and OCSF classes, and adopted network devices as device entities.

Source: src/archon/connect/integration-connector.ts

withIntegrationsfunction
function withIntegrations(base: Connector, ext: Extension): Connector

Give a connector the kinds it can also be (spec 109 FR-007): its own observation first, unchanged, then the integrations' reads on the same transport (a GET the connector sent is not sent again), adding what the connector did not write (records of a new label, assets, posture facts, gaps) and nothing it did. A failure of the integrations' reads is a gap: the connector's own observation stands.
