jumpcloud

Spec 091: JumpCloud (directory, SSO, and device management), read-only: its users (active, suspended, and whether they have enrolled a second factor), its console administrators (and whether they use one), and its devices (operating system, serial number, disk encryption, last contact). Evidence for MFA (SCF IAC-37), least privilege (IAC-30), account management (IAC-08), and encryption at rest (CRY-07).

Credential: the API key of a dedicated administrator with JumpCloud's Read Only role (the key acts with that administrator's rights).
