register

Spec 080: the risk register, as TypeScript. A fleet keeps it in fleets/<fleet>/risks.ts, one RiskDef per risk, so a change to a risk is a commit, reviewed and signed like any other.

Likelihood and impact use the qualitative scale of NIST SP 800-30 Rev 1 (Guide for Conducting Risk Assessments, Appendices G and H: very-low, low, moderate, high, very-high), and the risk level is that publication's Table I-2. Treatment uses the four standard risk responses (NIST SP 800-39 section 3.3 and ISO 31000: accept, mitigate, transfer, avoid). The scale, the table, and the treatments are IFC TypeScript's (kernel assess: RISK_LEVELS, RISK_MATRIX, riskLevel, Treatment); the register's own fields (an owner's work email, SCF controls, accepted risks, review days) are Stolos's.
