controls

Spec 077: people controls, on the control SDK (spec 078). Each is an estate control: its SQL reads the people and accounts of the estate graph, and its assertion compares them with what the fleet keeps in Git (roles, reviews, policies, acknowledgements, training, screening), which PeopleFacts carries. A leaver with an active account is the estate's own leavers control, reused, not repeated.

Source: src/archon/people/controls.ts
