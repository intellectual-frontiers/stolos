snmp

Spec 069: a small SNMPv3 client, read-only by construction: it sends only GET and GETNEXT, always with authentication and privacy (authPriv): HMAC-SHA-96 or HMAC-192-SHA-256 (RFC 3414, RFC 7860) and AES-128-CFB (RFC 3826), with the user-based security model's engine discovery and time synchronization. No SNMPv1 or v2c (community strings travel in the clear) and no SET.

In TypeScript, so the PC needs no net-snmp tools (Windows included).

Source: src/archon/device/snmp.ts
