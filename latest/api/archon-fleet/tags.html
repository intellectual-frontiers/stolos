tags

Spec 108-tags: the fleet's tags at work. Reads fleets/<fleet>/tags.ts (the definitions), gathers every machine (the managed servers and workstations, the adopted machines, the controller PC) with the facts rules and groups look at and the tags a person gave it (decisions.ts of a managed host, machine.ts of an adopted one), and resolves each machine's tags with their origins (resolveTags). The commands, the question to every machine, the compliance view and both consoles read tags from here.

Source: src/archon/fleet/tags.ts
