record

Spec 095-proposals: recording mode and reminders. archon record "nginx for the app", then the work, then archon record done: everything observed in between is one intent, with that reason, ready for archon propose --intent <id>. And so nothing waits forever, the daily report and archon doctor count drift not proposed and proposals not decided; after 3 days the daily report mails a reminder (at most every 3 days).

Source: src/archon/proposal/record.ts

Waitinginterface
interface Waiting

What is waiting: drift not proposed, proposals not decided, and whether a reminder is due.

MemberTypeWhat it is
unproposednumberDrift items not proposed (nor local, nor rejected).
oldestUnproposed?stringWhen the oldest of them was first seen (ISO).
waitingnumberProposals sent from here and not decided in Git.
oldestWaiting?stringWhen the oldest of them was sent (ISO).
duebooleanSomething has waited more than 3 days.

Count what waits on a person.

waitingLinefunction

One line: "3 change(s) outside Stolos not proposed (oldest 2026-11-10); 1 proposal waiting".

remindfunction
function remind(host: HostDef, w: Waiting, dir?: string, now?: Date, send?: ((ev: Event, host: string) => Promise<string>) | undefined): Promise<boolean>

The daily reminder: when something has waited more than 3 days and no reminder went out in the last 3 days, mail one (root only). Returns whether it was sent.
