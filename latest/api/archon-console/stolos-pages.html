stolos-pages

Spec 066: the Stolos Console's pages, for the person who runs the fleet from their PC: the fleet at a glance, every machine (managed, adopted, in a cloud), the clouds, one SQL question to every machine, the changes waiting in the repository and who may approve them, and every stolos command as a form. Each page says what it is for and draws, from the fleet's real state, how the pieces connect.

Source: src/archon/console/stolos-pages.ts
