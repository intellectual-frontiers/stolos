layout

Specs 065 and 066: both consoles' frame. A top bar (Archon on the left, the fleet with Stolos in the middle, the signed-in person on the right), a menu of pages, and the page. Datastar makes parts of a page live; Mermaid draws the diagrams (from the page's own data), loaded only on pages that have one, and drawn again when Datastar swaps one in.

Source: src/archon/console/layout.ts

interface Frame

What every page shows besides its body.

MemberTypeWhat it is
product"archon" | "stolos""archon" (one machine) or "stolos" (the fleet).
titlestringThe page's title (also the tab's).
subtitlestringUnder the product's name: the machine and its OS, or the PC.
fleet{ id: string; repo: string; }The fleet and its repository, in the middle.
user{ name: string; user: string; role: string; }The person signed in: name, username, and role in words.
navNavItem[]The menu.
currentstringThe current page's href.
scope?{ label: string; href: string; }Spec 082: the provider's client this page is for, and where the list of clients is.
engine?{ running: string; newer?: string | undefined; href: string; }Spec 099: the engine running here, and a newer release when there is one, with where to update it (the Stolos Console runs stolos update; the Archon Console explains it).
