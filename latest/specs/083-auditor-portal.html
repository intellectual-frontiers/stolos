Auditor portal

083

083-auditor-portal

Issue intellectual-frontiers/stolos#25: a read-only, time-boxed view for an external auditor: the controls in scope; populations and samples drawn by the tool; each sample's evidence, verifiable in place (spec 072); requests and responses kept as the audit trail.

#Why

An external auditor tests a control by taking its population (every leaver of the period, every change, every active account), drawing a sample, and examining each sample's evidence. Today the company answers with screenshots and spreadsheets the auditor cannot check, and the auditor cannot tell whether the population was complete or the sample was chosen. Stolos already gathers a period's evidence into a signed, hash-chained bundle (spec 072). The portal takes the populations from that bundle, lets the auditor's own seed choose the sample by a published method they can redo, shows each sample's evidence with its hash and its place in the signed manifest, and keeps every request and response as a signed commit. The auditor verifies each sample without asking the company for a screenshot.

#Requirements

FR-001

An engagement is fleets/<fleet>/audits/<id>/engagement.ts (src/archon/audit/engagement.ts), a typed TypeScript literal (Engagement, exported from @intellectual-frontiers/stolos/archon/audit/mod) that Stolos reads without running it: the audit firm and its people, the period, the frameworks, the controls in scope (each with the id the auditor uses, a title, optional SCF ids, and the population it is tested on), the pinned evidence bundle (file name, SHA-256, manifest head), the last day of access (access.expires, UTC, the day included), when and by which signer it was opened, and when it was closed.

FR-002

stolos audit open, audit bundle, audit access, audit close write it; audit request, audit respond, audit sample add to its trail. Each makes one commit of those files only, signed with Git's signing key (git commit -S; ssh-keygen asks the passphrase in the terminal), whose message carries a trailer Audit-Entry: <path> sha256:<SHA-256 of the file> for each file: the signature covers the exact text. Nothing is pushed. A hand edit that is not committed is refused, and a closed engagement takes no more entries. audit bundle accepts only a bundle signed by a fleet signer whose period covers the engagement's, and copies it into this PC's evidence directory.

FR-003

Populations (src/archon/audit/populations.ts): each is a typed query over one bundle's items (BundleView), never over this PC's live state. change (each commit item), leavers (each person an HR observation shows terminated with an end date in the period; the evidence is the first observation that shows it, and the period's last observation of each system that listed an account with their email), mfa (each active non-service account in each system's last observation), devices (devices in the last observations, and adopted machines' last observations), repositories, access-reviews (each people item and its approving commit), verify (each verify item). A unit has a unique id, facts, and its evidence items by SHA-256. A population's hash is sha256("stolos-audit-population/1\n<id>\n<manifest head>\n" + "<unit id>\t<sha256,...>\n" per unit sorted by id). Each query is documented in the guide and on the portal.

FR-004

Samples (src/archon/audit/sample.ts): the auditor gives the seed (any one line of text: a number, or a value from a public randomness source they chose; Stolos only accepts it) and the sample size (theirs to choose; Stolos reproduces no sample size table). The method, stolos-srswor-sha256/1, is simple random sampling without replacement by random sort keys: each unit's key is sha256(seed + LF + unit id) in lowercase hex, the units are ordered by key (then id), and the sample is the first n. The sample, seed, method, size, population size, and population hash are recorded in the trail. The guide and the package's README show the shell that redraws it.

FR-005

The trail (src/archon/audit/trail.ts): audits/<id>/trail/<NNNN>-<kind>.md, numbered in order, kinds request, response, sample, note; front matter with one line of JSON per key (seq, kind, at, by, control, re, sample), then the text. A request's by is one of the engagement's auditors; a response's re names an earlier entry.

FR-006

Checking (src/archon/audit/case.ts): an engagement is read as committed (HEAD) and checked: engagement.ts and each entry are the text their last commit's trailer vouches for, and that commit's SSH signature verifies against the fleet's signers; the pinned bundle is found, has the pinned SHA-256 and head, and verifyBundle runs on it; each control's population is computed; each sample is redrawn from its seed against the population's hash. The same code checks an export package.

FR-007

The portal (src/archon/audit/portal.ts), in the Stolos Console on 127.0.0.1:

  • stolos audit portal <id> starts the console and prints only an audit link (/audit/launch?t=...): it works once, for two minutes, and becomes a session (the console's cookie) in the group audit:<id> that may read only /audit/<id>/ and /v1/audit/<id>/, with GET and HEAD, for at most four hours. Every other route answers 403; anything but GET and HEAD answers 405. A fleet operator's own session sees the portal too.
  • Every request is refused (410) once the engagement is past access.expires or closed.
  • Pages: the engagement (firm, period, controls with population sizes and hashes, the bundle's verification); each control's population (every unit, its facts, its evidence) with a draw preview from a seed (not recorded); each recorded sample (each unit's items: SHA-256, kind, name, manifest index and chain link, the bundle's verification and the redraw); each item (its text and manifest entry, and a download); the trail in order with each entry's commit and signer; downloads of the bundle and the export package.
  • The same as JSON under /v1/audit/<id>/ (/, controls/<control>, samples/<seq>, items/<sha256>, items/<sha256>/raw, trail, verify, bundle.zip, export.zip), listed in the console's OpenAPI document (src/archon/audit/openapi.ts, merged into spec 094's).
FR-008

Sharing with a remote auditor: the console answers only on this PC, so the company sends the export package (stolos audit export <id>, src/archon/audit/package.ts): the bundle, engagement.ts and every trail entry each with the raw commit that recorded it, the fleet's signers, populations.json, the portal as one static page, index.json (every file's SHA-256), and a README for checking by hand. stolos audit verify <package> [--signers] checks it offline with the same code (FR-006) and names each problem. Nothing new is signed; the package is not time-boxed (sending it is the company's decision). An auditor on site uses the portal link on the company's PC.

FR-009

Commands in the registry, on the PC: the writing ones (audit open, bundle, access, request, respond, sample, close) and audit portal and audit export are terminal only (noMcp); audit list, audit show, audit verify are read-only and in the console group "Audits".

FR-010

The guide audit (src/archon/audit/mod.ts), registered in src/archon/core/guides.ts.

#Success criteria

SC-001success criterion

tests/audit083.test.ts, with real SSH keys and signed commits:

  • engagement.ts round-trips, and malformed ones are named;
  • open and close are signed commits whose trailer vouches for engagement.ts; a second open, a second close, an entry after closing, and a change over an uncommitted hand edit are refused;
  • the populations of a fixture bundle: the leaver with their HR and directory evidence, the active accounts (no service account), every commit of the period and none after; hashes are stable; every unit's items are in the bundle with their SHA-256;
  • the same seed draws the same sample, another seed another, the population's order does not matter, and the key is what sha256sum computes;
  • a recorded sample redraws from its seed; each unit's items, through the portal's JSON, match their hashes and their manifest entries, and the bundle verifies; a changed sample entry is named;
  • an audit-scoped session reads its engagement only (403 elsewhere, 405 for writes), its link works once, and its JSON is valid against the OpenAPI document;
  • an expired or closed engagement is refused, in HTML and JSON;
  • the export package verifies offline; a changed request, a changed bundle item, and someone else's signers are named.
SC-002success criterion

Existing tests pass; the OpenAPI document stays valid OpenAPI 3.1.

#What CPA-firm acceptance still needs

Building this does not make the evidence format accepted. The issue asks for it to be done with two or three CPA firms; that is work for people, and it remains:

  1. Walk two or three firms (SOC 2 practitioners, ideally one that also does ISO 27001) through a real engagement on a pilot fleet: the portal on site, and the export package remotely.
  2. Ask each firm's methodology owner to accept, in writing, (a) stolos-srswor-sha256/1 as a random selection method for attribute sampling, with the auditor choosing the seed and the size under their own sampling guidance, and (b) the population definitions of FR-003 as complete for the controls they map to (the leavers population depends on the HR system's records; firms may ask for a reconciliation to payroll).
  3. Ask whether they accept the bundle's signature (the company's signer) and the commit trailers as evidence of integrity, or want a third-party timestamp (RFC 3161) on the manifest; record the answer and add the timestamp if asked.
  4. Ask how they want requests recorded: the company records the auditor's request verbatim (FR-005); a firm may prefer to sign its own requests (an auditor key in engagement.ts, verified like a signer's), which is a small change here.
  5. Capture what each firm still asked for as a screenshot, and turn each into a population or an item kind, until none is asked for.
  6. Publish the accepted format (the package layout, the method, the population definitions) as a versioned document the firms can cite in their workpapers.

#Decisions to review

  • One bundle per engagement (re-pinning replaces it; samples drawn from the old bundle then name a different population hash, and the check says so).
  • The company records the auditor's requests; the auditor does not sign them (see above).
  • The portal refuses everyone, operators included, once the engagement has expired or closed; operators use stolos audit show.
  • An audit session lasts at most four hours, besides the engagement's last day.
  • The export package is not time-boxed.
  • The SCF ids each population usually tests are those Stolos already declares (CHG-03, HRS-12, IAC-04, IAC-37, AST-04, CRY-07, IAC-12, MON-02); the auditor's own criteria ids are free text.

#Out of scope

  • Serving the portal beyond this PC (a public host, a tunnel): the export package is the remote path.
  • Sample size tables and statistical evaluation of exceptions: the auditor's methodology.
  • Populations defined by a fleet's own control packs (a later spec may let a pack declare one).
