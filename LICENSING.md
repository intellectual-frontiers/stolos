you may read, copy, change, and test it freely, and production use needs a commercial license. Each
version becomes open source (Apache License 2.0) four years after it is first published.

This page explains the license in plain words. The [LICENSE](LICENSE) is what binds; if this page
and the LICENSE disagree, the LICENSE wins.

## Free, without asking

Non-production use, by anyone, including companies:

- **Evaluating** Stolos: trying it on test machines, virtual machines, labs, and proofs of concept.
- **Testing and development**: running its tests, its CI, its end-to-end labs; building on it.
- **Learning and teaching**: courses, workshops, demonstrations, and writing about it.
- **Security research**: reviewing the code, and reporting what you find.
- **Contributing** changes back to this repository.
- Reading, copying, modifying, and redistributing the source, with this license kept on every copy.

## Needs a commercial license

Production use, that is, relying on Stolos for real work:

- Managing, observing, or checking machines, devices, or cloud and SaaS accounts that serve your
  business (or your customers').
- Relying on its compliance evidence, reports, or exports for an audit, a customer, or a regulator.
- Offering it, or a product built on it, to others: hosted, managed, embedded, or resold.

For a commercial license, open an issue at
[intellectual-frontiers/stolos](https://github.com/intellectual-frontiers/stolos) (or ask the person
who gave you Stolos) and say how you would use it.

## Versions, and what becomes open source

- **v0.90.0 and later** are under the BSL. Each version converts to the Apache License 2.0 on the
  fourth anniversary of its first public release: from then on, that version is open source for
  everyone.
- **v0.89.0 and earlier** were released under the Apache License 2.0 and stay under it: tags and the
  code in them are never moved or relicensed.

pull request that you agree to this.
