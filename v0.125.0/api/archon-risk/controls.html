controls

Spec 080: the register and the vendors in the estate graph, and the controls over them, written on the control SDK (spec 078) like any other pack. addRiskData loads the register (risk), the accepted risks (poam_item), and the vendors (organization parties with the party_role supplier, their edges, and their data_classification) into an estate; RISK_PACK's controls then ask it with SQL:

controlfails or warns whenSCF
review-duean open risk is past its next review (fails)RSK-14, RSK-13.2
owner-on-rostera risk's owner is not an active person on the roster (warns)RSK-11
accepted-currentan accepted risk is past its date (fails); one is unregisteredRSK-16.1
vendors-triageda discovered vendor is not triaged in vendors.ts (warns)TPM-08, TPM-09

Source: src/archon/risk/controls.ts
