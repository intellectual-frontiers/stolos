group

Spec 095-proposals: change sessions. Observations are grouped by sudo session (the same person, each command within 30 minutes of the previous) into one intent, so "installed nginx, edited its site, enabled it, and opened 443" is one item to decide, not four. A recording (archon record) wins: everything seen while it ran is one intent, with its reason.

Source: src/archon/proposal/group.ts
