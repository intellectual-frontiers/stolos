archon/audit/package.ts

Spec 083: the export package, for an auditor who works remotely or offline. stolos audit export <id> writes one ZIP an auditor can check with nothing but the package (and, by hand, sha256sum, ssh-keygen, and a shell):

In the packageWhat it is
bundle/<file>.zipthe evidence bundle (spec 072), unchanged: signed, hash-chained
repo/fleets/<fleet>/audits/<id>/engagement.tsthe engagement, and .commit: the raw commit that recorded it
repo/.../trail/<n>-<kind>.mdeach request, response, and sample, each with its .commit
allowed_signersthe fleet's signers when the package was made
populations.jsoneach control's population: units, facts, evidence items, hash
portal.htmlthe portal as one static page
index.jsonthe package's format, and every file's SHA-256
README.txthow to check it

Nothing in the package needs a new signature: the bundle is signed, every commit is signed, and the populations and samples are computed again from the bundle and the seeds. The package is not time-boxed: giving it to someone is the company's decision; the portal is.

flowchart LR
  case["the engagement, checked"] --> zip["export package (ZIP)"]
  zip --> v["stolos audit verify: offline"]
  v --> b["verifyBundle"] & c["each commit: trailer and signature"] & p["populations recomputed"] & s["samples redrawn"]
