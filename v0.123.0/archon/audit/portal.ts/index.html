archon/audit/portal.ts

Spec 083: the auditor portal, served by the Stolos Console on 127.0.0.1.

  • stolos audit portal <id> starts the console and prints a one-time link (/audit/launch?t=..., two minutes, once) that becomes an audit-scoped session: it sees only /audit/<id>/ and /v1/audit/<id>/, read-only (GET and HEAD), for at most {@link AUDIT_SESSION_MS}, and every request is refused once the engagement has expired or been closed.
  • A fleet operator signed in to the console sees the same pages.
