archon/risk/controls.ts

Spec 080: the register and the vendors in the estate graph, and the controls over them, written on the control SDK (spec 078) like any other pack. {@link addRiskData} loads the register (risk), the accepted risks (poam_item), and the vendors (organization parties with the party_role supplier, their edges, and their data_classification) into an estate; {@link RISK_PACK}'s controls then ask it with SQL:

controlfails or warns whenSCF
review-duean open risk is past its next review (fails)RSK-14, RSK-13.2
owner-on-rostera risk's owner is not an active person on the roster (warns)RSK-11
accepted-currentan accepted risk is past its date (fails); one is unregisteredRSK-16.1
vendors-triageda discovered vendor is not triaged in vendors.ts (warns)TPM-08, TPM-09
flowchart LR
  risks["risks.ts"] & accepted["controls/accepted.ts, adopted machines"] & vendors["discoverVendors + vendors.ts"] --> add["addRiskData"]
  add --> estate[("estate graph: risk, poam_item, organization, data_classification")]
  estate --> pack["RISK_PACK (estate controls)"]
  pack --> comp["stolos compliance, stolos risks check"]
