archon/fleet/decisions.ts

Spec 047-intent-and-identity (Article 25): what operators decided for a host (which opt-ins are on, and whether their data is durable) lives in Git, in fleets/<id>/hosts/<host>/decisions.ts, written by stolos decide on a workstation and approved by a signed push. Such a decision wins over the host's own record (archon.sql), survives a re-install, and tells the boot machine whether a host holds durable data even when it is offline.
