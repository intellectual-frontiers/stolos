archon/compliance/freshness.ts

Spec 073: evidence freshness as a control. Every source of evidence has a validation cadence (how often it is observed) and a service level (how late an observation may be before it no longer counts): managed servers verify every 15 minutes (the reconcile timer); adopted machines, devices, and connected accounts are observed daily; a connector may declare its own cadence; and a fleet may override any of them in fleet.ts (compliance.cadence). The cadences are modeled on FedRAMP 20x's persistent (continuous) validation, which expects machine-based validation every 3 to 7 days ({@link FEDRAMP_20X_VALIDATION}): no service level here is ever longer than 7 days.

Evidence older than its service level is stale (IFC TypeScript's assess.freshness, the service level as its cadence). Stale evidence is a failing check: it fails its own controls and SCF MON-02 (Continuous Monitoring), and its fix is the command that observes the source again. Each control shows the age of its newest and oldest evidence.

flowchart LR
  obs["An observation: verify, host observe, device observe, estate observe"] -- "observedAt" --> ev["Evidence"]
  cad["Cadence and service level: default, connector, fleet.ts"] --> age{"older than its service level?"}
  ev --> age
  age -- "no" --> ctl["Its SCF controls, as observed"]
  age -- "yes: stale" --> fail["A failing check: its controls and MON-02"]
  fail --> fix["Fix: observe it again"]

typeDuration

A length of time: minutes, hours, or days ("15m", "6h", "1d").

interfaceCadenceDef

export interface CadenceDef {
  /** How often it is observed ("15m", "1d"). */
  every: Duration;
  /**
   * The service level: past this age its evidence is stale. Default: twice
   * `every`. Never more than 7 days (FedRAMP 20x's longest validation
   * interval): a longer one is capped.
   */
  staleAfter?: Duration;
}

How often a source is observed, and how late an observation may be.

typeCadenceSource

export type CadenceSource = "managed" | "adopted" | "device" | "cloud";

The sources of evidence that are observed on a schedule (the others are read live from Git).

interfaceCadenceOverrides

export interface CadenceOverrides {
  /** Managed servers and workstations (their verify). */
  managed?: CadenceDef;
  /** Adopted machines (`stolos host observe`). */
  adopted?: CadenceDef;
  /** SNMP devices (`stolos device observe`). */
  device?: CadenceDef;
  /** Every connected account without a cadence of its own (`stolos estate observe`). */
  cloud?: CadenceDef;
  /** One connector, by its id: wins over the connector's own cadence and over `cloud`. */
  connectors?: Record<string, CadenceDef>;
}

A fleet's own cadences (compliance.cadence in fleets/<fleet>/fleet.ts): per source, and per connector by its id.

Example

import type { CadenceOverrides } from "@intellectual-frontiers/stolos/archon/compliance/freshness";

export const cadence: CadenceOverrides = {
  managed: { every: "1h" },
  connectors: { bamboohr: { every: "7d", staleAfter: "7d" } },
};

functionapplyFreshness

export function applyFreshness(ev: Evidence[], c: Cadences, now = Date.now()): Evidence[]

Hold each piece of evidence to its service level: one older than it is stale, and becomes a failing check of its own controls and of MON-02 (Continuous Monitoring), with what it said kept in stale.was and the command that observes it again as its fix. Evidence without a result ("unknown": nothing was observed) and evidence read live is left as it is.
