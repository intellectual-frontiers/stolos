archon/connect/gitlab.ts

Spec 091: GitLab (GitLab.com or a self-managed GitLab), read-only, for one top-level group: whether it requires two-factor authentication, its members (who are owners, and who has 2FA, which GitLab shows a group owner's token), and its projects (private, internal, or public, whether the default branch is protected, how many approvals a merge request needs, and secret push protection). Evidence for change management (SCF CHG-03), MFA (IAC-37), least privilege (IAC-30), and scanning (VPM-12).

Credential: a group access token (or a personal access token) with the read_api scope only. GitLab shows members' 2FA only to group owners, so a token with the Owner role reads more; with any lower role 2FA is a gap.
