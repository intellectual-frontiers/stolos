archon/connect/labels.ts

Spec 091: the labels connectors fill, declared once (spec 089). Each is a term from MITRE D3FEND or OCSF, and each record a connector reads carries one: user_account, code_repository, person (an HR roster), and hardware_device become the estate's accounts, repositories, people, and devices; the others become nodes of the estate graph as they are.
