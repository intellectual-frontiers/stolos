Spec 095-proposals (Article 25): what operators decided about a host's proposals lives in Git, in fleets/<fleet>/hosts/<host>/proposals.ts, written by stolos proposal accept and reject and approved by a signed push. The host reads it after it pulls: a decided proposal ends its holds, a rejected one leaves its unmanaged items flagged, and accepted local drift stays quiet until its date. It is read as a literal, never run.
