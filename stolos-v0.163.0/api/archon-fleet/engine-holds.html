engine-holds

Staged rollouts (spec 115-compatibility FR-013 to FR-015): hold one host, or a group of hosts chosen by tags, at an engine tag while the rest of the fleet moves. A hold is a decision typed in the model (enginePin in a host's host.ts, engineHolds in fleet.ts): the tag in the form stolos-v<major>.<minor>.<patch>, a reason and an end date, at most 180 days away. A host's own hold wins over a group's, and the first group that chooses a host over the later ones; a hold past its date is ignored and said.

This release records, validates, shows and checks a hold (git ls-remote --tags of the engine's repository, with a time limit); it does not install the held engine on the machine: the words say so, and specs/115-compatibility/tasks.md holds the design and the blocker.

Whether a hold has ended (its day is before today). A hold with no valid date counts as ended.

A hold in plain words: held at stolos-v0.150.0 (fleet pin stolos-v0.152.0); and, until the install side exists, that it is not enforced.

engineHoldProblemsfunction
function engineHoldProblems(fleet: HoldFleet, hosts: readonly Pick<HoldHost, "enginePin" | "name">[], set: Pick<TagSet<Readonly<Record<string, TagDimensionDef>>, string>, "dimensions"> | undefined, now?: Date): Problem[]

The problems of every hold of a fleet, for stolos build (spec 115-compatibility FR-013): a hold that is not typed right, a group's expression that does not parse or names a tag the fleet does not define. Whether a tag exists in the engine's repository: yes, no, or not checked (GitHub unreachable, or a timeout).

The URL git ls-remote asks about a pin's engine: GitHub for a github: source, the URL itself for a git+ source.

Whether each tag exists in the engine's repository, by git ls-remote --tags --refs <url> (spec 115-compatibility FR-014), with a time limit. An unreachable repository says not-checked for every tag, never missing.

The newer of two engine tags, for a sentence that says which way a hold goes: older, newer or same than the fleet's pin.
