freshness

Spec 073: evidence freshness as a control. Every source of evidence has a validation cadence (how often it is observed) and a service level (how late an observation may be before it no longer counts): managed servers verify every 15 minutes (the reconcile timer); adopted machines, devices, and connected accounts are observed daily; a connector may declare its own cadence; and a fleet may override any of them in fleet.ts (compliance.cadence). The cadences are modeled on FedRAMP 20x's persistent (continuous) validation, which expects machine-based validation every 3 to 7 days (FEDRAMP_20X_VALIDATION): no service level here is ever longer than 7 days.

Evidence older than its service level is stale (IFC TypeScript's assess.freshness, the service level as its cadence). Stale evidence is a failing check: it fails its own controls and SCF MON-02 (Continuous Monitoring), and its fix is the command that observes the source again. Each control shows the age of its newest and oldest evidence.

Source: src/archon/compliance/freshness.ts

CadenceDefinterface
interface CadenceDef

How often a source is observed, and how late an observation may be.

MemberTypeWhat it is
every`${number}d` | `${number}h` | `${number}m`How often it is observed ("15m", "1d").
staleAfter?`${number}d` | `${number}h` | `${number}m`The service level: past this age its evidence is stale. Default: twice every. Never more than 7 days (FedRAMP 20x's longest validation interval): a longer one is capped.
CadenceSourcetype
type CadenceSource = "managed" | "adopted" | "device" | "cloud";

The sources of evidence that are observed on a schedule (the others are read live from Git).

CadenceOverridesinterface
interface CadenceOverrides

A fleet's own cadences (compliance.cadence in fleets/<fleet>/fleet.ts): per source, and per connector by its id.

MemberTypeWhat it is
managed?CadenceDefManaged servers and workstations (their verify).
adopted?CadenceDefAdopted machines (stolos host observe).
device?CadenceDefSNMP devices (stolos device observe).
cloud?CadenceDefEvery connected account without a cadence of its own (stolos estate observe).
connectors?Record<string, CadenceDef>One connector, by its id: wins over the connector's own cadence and over cloud.

Verify servers hourly, and read the HR system weekly

FEDRAMP_20X_VALIDATIONconst
const FEDRAMP_20X_VALIDATION: { minDays: number; maxDays: number; title: string; url: string; }

FedRAMP 20x's persistent validation: cloud service providers validate their key security indicators with machines, continuously, at least every 3 to 7 days by impact level. Stolos's service levels are never longer than the longest of these.

DEFAULT_CADENCESconst
const DEFAULT_CADENCES: Record<CadenceSource, CadenceDef>

The default cadences: verify every 15 minutes, everything else daily.

When each piece of the fleet's evidence was observed, from what the Stolos Console knows: a managed machine's last pull (its reconcile run, which verifies) else its install; an adopted machine's or device's observation; a connector's observation (and the connector's id).

applyFreshnessfunction
function applyFreshness(ev: Evidence[], c: Cadences, now?: number): Evidence[]

Hold each piece of evidence to its service level: one older than it is stale, and becomes a failing check of its own controls and of MON-02 (Continuous Monitoring), with what it said kept in stale.was and the command that observes it again as its fix. Evidence without a result ("unknown": nothing was observed) and evidence read live is left as it is.
