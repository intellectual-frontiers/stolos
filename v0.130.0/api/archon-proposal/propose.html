propose

Spec 095-proposals: archon propose on a server. Anyone with sudo may propose (the owner's decision 3). It collects again, picks the items (all, by intent, or by key), applies each person's choice (adopt, revert, or keep local until a day), requires a written reason, builds and signs proposal.json with the host key, keeps a copy in /var/lib/archon/proposals/<id>/, records it in archon.sql, and mails it to the fleet's mailbox with a readable summary.

Source: src/archon/proposal/propose.ts

ProposeOptionsinterface
interface ProposeOptions

What archon propose was asked for.

MemberTypeWhat it is
all?booleanEvery item not proposed yet.
intent?stringIntents by id (newline-separated when repeated).
item?stringItems by key (newline-separated when repeated).
action?stringThe action for every chosen item (default adopt).
revert?stringItems to revert instead (keys, newline-separated).
local?stringItems to keep local instead (keys, newline-separated).
until?stringLocal: until this day (default 30 days from now).
reason?stringWhy (required, unless a recording gives it).
dryRun?booleanPrint proposal.json; sign, record, and send nothing.
json?boolean--json.
