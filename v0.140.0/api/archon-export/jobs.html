jobs

Spec 092's export jobs as the fleet declares them (estate.exports in fleets/<id>/fleet.ts, see ExportJobDef): checking them, matching their tenants, and reading what each needs at run time (the recipients' certificates, the sender, the credential's name), and the operating system's job for each (kernel jobs). Pure, apart from reading a certificate file.

Each job is one tenant's (or a few tenants') arrangement: its own kinds, its own recipients and sender, its own schedule. Nothing secret is in a job; the credential is named, and lives in this PC's secrets folder.

Source: src/archon/export/jobs.ts
