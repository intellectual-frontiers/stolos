summary

Spec 082: the cross-client summary. For each client, read separately in its own scope, only numbers and dates: how many controls pass, fail, are partial, have no evidence, or have stale evidence; how many changed since the last day (and how many got worse); when anything was last observed; how many POA&M items are open; and the date of its newest evidence bundle. No machine, account, check, finding, or person of any client is in it.

Source: src/archon/clients/summary.ts

ClientSummaryinterface
interface ClientSummary

One client's numbers.

MemberTypeWhat it is
clientstringThe client's id.
titlestringThe client's name, as the provider registered it.
readablebooleanWhether its checkout could be read; when not, the numbers are zero and problem says why.
problem?stringWhy it could not be read, in general words (never what the checkout holds).
controls{ pass: number; fail: number; partial: number; noEvidence: number; stale: number; }Controls by status; stale counts those with any stale evidence.
machinesnumberHow many machines the fleet knows (managed, adopted, devices, clouds).
drift?{ since: string; changed: number; worse: number; }Controls whose status changed since since (none: the history does not reach back).
lastObservation?stringWhen the newest evidence was observed (ISO), if any has a time.
poamOpennumberOpen POA&M items: failing checks, open risks in the register, open vulnerabilities.
evidenceBundle?stringThe day the newest evidence bundle on this PC was written (YYYY-MM-DD).
asOfstringWhen the numbers were taken (ISO).

readClientfunction
function readClient(c: Client, o?: { since?: string | undefined; now?: Date | undefined; }): Promise<ClientRead | { summary: ClientSummary; }>

Read one client, in its own scope: its facts, its compliance view, its drift since since (default one day), its register and vulnerabilities, and its newest bundle. A client that cannot be read gives unreadable, never an error.
