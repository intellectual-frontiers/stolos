commands

Spec 100: the stolos devices commands and the console's names.

  • stolos devices: the approved devices (access.ts) with this PC's last sign-in of each.
  • stolos devices approve <code> [--controller <url>] [--yes]: fetch a pending registration's public fields from the controller, show them, ask, write the device into access.ts with your own signer name as approvedBy, and send it (stolos apply, signed by your key).
  • stolos devices revoke <id> [--yes]: remove it from access.ts and send that (signed).
  • consoleNamesSetup: what stolos console --lan and fleet init do first: propose the names, ask, and write them into fleet.ts.

Source: src/archon/access/commands.ts
