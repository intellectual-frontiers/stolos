Trust Center from signed evidence

081

081-trust-center

Issue intellectual-frontiers/stolos#22.

#Why

A company's customers ask how it is doing on SOC 2, HIPAA, or the controls they care about, and usually get a page someone typed. A Trust Center built from signed evidence answers instead with what was checked: each claim backed by a check a reader can verify, with how fresh its evidence is, and nothing failing or stale hidden.

#Requirements

FR-001

stolos trust build --bundle <zip> [--out <dir>] [--frameworks soc2,hipaa,...] [--public|--gated] [--on-request] [--key <file>] [--signers <file>] [--contact <link>] verifies the bundle first (spec 072's verifyBundle; --signers replaces the bundle's own) and refuses it, naming each problem, when anything is wrong. Nothing is written for a refused bundle.

FR-002

The page is built from the bundle alone (never this PC's live state): the company (the fleet's title), the period, the signer, when it was signed, the bundle's SHA-256; the checks and their SCF controls from the bundle's OSCAL assessment results (spec 071); each check's observation time from the bundle's other items behind it (spec 092's bundleItemsFor: verify results, observations, commits; never the OSCAL document or signers.ts, which are stamped at export). A bundle without assessment results is refused.

FR-003

Framework requirements come through the SCF Council's mappings (STRM) of the SCF release the bundle's OSCAL back-matter names, from that catalog when this PC has it; otherwise NIST SP 800-53 only, from the bundle's own findings, and every other framework is listed as not shown with the reason. --frameworks naming one that cannot be shown is refused.

FR-004

Freshness (spec 073): each check is held to its source's default service level as of the bundle's signing; a stale check fails, and its control and MON-02 say how many were stale. Each control shows the age of its newest and oldest evidence and how many checks have no time. A bundle signed more than 7 days before the page is built (FedRAMP 20x's longest validation interval) makes every status stale, with a banner; the page's script shows the same banner when a reader opens a page that has aged past it. Failing and stale counts are at the top of the page.

FR-005

Every claim links to what backs it by SHA-256: gated, each full-bundle item (evidence/objects/<sha256>); public, the control's item in the summary bundle (and the full bundle's item hashes as text). The page says how to check them: stolos evidence verify, or sha256sum and ssh-keygen as the bundle's README says.

FR-006

Public (the default) shows the company, period, signer, statuses, counts, ages, and item hashes, kinds, and times; it leaves out subjects (hostnames, accounts, the repository), check titles, what each check found, item names, and people's names and emails. It publishes a summary bundle it derives (stolos-evidence/1, items of kind summary: an index and one document per control with exactly format, id, name, how, status, checks, counts, observed, backedBy), signed through a signing callback (072's sshSigner, --key) and refused unless its signer is the full bundle's; its allowed_signers holds only that signer's principal and key (no comment). With --on-request it publishes no bundle and says the evidence is available on request.

FR-007

Gated shows everything in the bundle (subjects, findings, item names) and publishes the full bundle, unpacked. Stolos builds no sign-in: the guide documents hosting it behind the company's own access (Cloudflare Access in front of Cloudflare Pages, or any access proxy). The page asks not to be indexed.

FR-008

The page is one static HTML file: its CSS and script inline and pinned by SHA-256 in a Content-Security-Policy with default-src 'none' (no request leaves it), system fonts, light and dark, readable at phone width (tables scroll in their own box), landmarks, a skip link, table headers, and statuses in words as well as color. The only outside link is the company's own --contact (https: or mailto:).

FR-009

--out (default: the state directory's trust/public or trust/gated) is replaced only when empty or marked by an earlier build (.stolos-trust). The command is terminal only (noMcp), offered on the PC.

#Success criteria

SC-001success criterion

tests/trust081.test.ts, from a real bundle (signed commits observing two machines, a verify, OSCAL, a throwaway ed25519 key): the public page shows failing and stale controls as such, links every claim to an item in the published summary bundle whose bytes hash to the link, carries no subject, hostname, email, finding, or item name in any published file, is well formed, and names no URL but the company's; the gated page links every item behind every control; a tampered bundle, a summary signed by another key, and an unreachable framework are refused; a stale bundle shows the banner; --on-request publishes nothing.

#Later

A CycloneDX attestation (spec 092) carried in the bundle as an item of its own, so its claims are the page's claims; a fleet's own cadences (compliance.cadence) carried in the bundle; RFC 3161 timestamps.
